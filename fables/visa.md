> **ON THE WORD.**
>
> ***Visa*** *is the neuter plural of the past participle of* videre. *Things which have been seen.*
>
> *The word entered administration on the back of a longer phrase — a paper endorsed* charta visa, *the document has been looked at — and then the phrase wore away and left the participle standing on its own, the way phrases do.*
>
> *A visa certifies nothing whatever about the person carrying it. It makes no claim about their character, their intentions, their history, or their right to exist. It certifies one fact only, about somebody else: that an official, on a date, at a post, looked.*
>
> *Every jurisdiction on the continent has since built an enormous apparatus on top of that participle. Four have tried to replace it with a better word. All four came back to the participle.*

## 620 m

<font color=royalblue>"Aemeath."</font>

<font color=mistyrose>"I'm having a moment about infrastructure and I'd like it respected."</font>

The terraces started at about six hundred metres and went up the valley sides on both hands, cut in steps a metre and a half deep, following the contour, going round the shoulders of the hills in curves that had been surveyed by nobody at all and were perfect.

The lower bays had water standing in them already, silver, flat, holding the sky, in shapes like the growth rings of an enormous tree seen from above.

There were people on them. Distant, bent, in blue and grey, in ones and twos, doing something to a bund with a mattock.

Mei rode past all of it slowly, on the near bike, with her visor up.

## 1,180 m

They came round the shoulder above the fourth terrace stack at half past two in the afternoon, and the road did a long right-hand bend with a drop on the outside, and the valley opened, and there they were.

The word *mountains* had been out of use for a while.

The frostlands are flat. The Reaches are corrugated. The last real mountains any of them had stood in front of had been on the other side of an underground winter and a summer before that, and the eye forgets. The eye forgets in a specific way: it keeps the word and loses the *quantity*, so that when the thing arrives again it arrives as an error.

Four ranks of them, going up and east. The near rank green-black and creased. The second rank grey with the snowline on it like a tidemark. The third rank white all the way down and the size of an argument. And behind the third rank, so far back that the air had gone blue in between and the whole thing had stopped looking like rock and started looking like a decision somebody had made about the sky, the fourth rank, with cloud coming off the tops of it in a long horizontal banner going east, and in it, in the third notch from the left, a saddle with a road on it.

The truck stopped on its own account.

<font color=skyblue>"...Oh."</font>

<font color=mistyrose>"Ma."</font>

<font color=moccasin>"Yeah."</font>

<font color=mistyrose>"That's — how far is that?"</font>

<font color=moccasin>"Which one?"</font>

<font color=mistyrose>"The white one. The big white one behind the other big white one."</font>

<font color=royalblue>"Sixty-one kilometres to the ridge line and it stands about four thousand two hundred, and I have the chart, and I checked it four times this morning, and I have been waiting since the fourth ford to be asked."</font>

<font color=mistyrose>"*Sixty-one kilometres.*"</font>

<font color=moccasin>"Mm."</font>

<font color=mistyrose>"I can *see* something that's sixty-one kilometres away."</font>

Cartethyia stayed where she was.

<font color=coral>"I have looked at four hundred people in the last two years and I have got a colour and a shape and a smear where the middle should be, and I have built a whole life on gait and breathing and the weight of a footstep and it *works*, and I would not trade it, and I have said all of that out loud to a great many people and meant it."</font> A breath. <font color=coral>"And that is sixty-one kilometres of rock and I can see the *seams.*"</font>

## 1,410 m

The rest stop below the pass was one concrete building, a tiled counter, a canopy, a water tank, a hot-water dispenser with a queue for it, eleven vehicles, and a mart.

The mart was four metres by nine and it contained more different foods than the *Lucky Bird* had carried in twelve days.

They stood in the doorway for a moment like a group entering a cathedral.

<font color=skyblue>"Oh, this is the best day of my *life.*"</font>

---

**Tea eggs**, in a vat, in a liquor the colour of a chestnut, cracked all over so the pattern went in. Mei bought six and ate two and made everybody take one.

**Century egg**, sliced, on cold tofu, with soy and ginger and a scatter of spring onion. Amy looked at it for four full seconds.

<font color=mistyrose>"It's *grey.*"</font>

The incident occurred at eleven minutes past three and involved the phrase.

Iuno had been standing at the end of the counter for some time with a cup of hot water and a hand flat on her own midsection and an expression of scientific detachment, and had said, to nobody, twice, *"I am monitoring a situation."*

Mei had eleven days of a language tape in her, and about four phrases of it reliably, and one of them was ranked first by frequency of household use, and it came out of her mouth with total confidence and reasonably good tones:

<font color=moccasin>"My friend is unwell."</font>

## 1,940 m

Snow on both sides, cut back four metres by a machine into a corridor with blue in the walls of it. Wind coming over the saddle from the east with the smell of a whole different country in it. ## KELVET — 41ST IN LINE

The post at Kelvet was on the eastern side at fourteen hundred metres, in a place with a Royan name and a Ministry flag, and it had been Royan-staffed until the spring and had been staffed by the Ministry since, and every driver on that road had an opinion about that and about four of them would tell you.

The queue was forty-one vehicles long at two in the afternoon and it moved at about six an hour.

The *Lucky Bird* joined the back of it behind a livestock truck.

---

The plates:

A Mingting freight consortium unit, blue on white, four digits and a consortium mark, with a crew of two who had been through Kelvet nine times this season and had opinions.

Two Huanglong provincial trucks, red on cream, running empty westbound and waiting for the alternate convoy.

A pilgrimage bus, forty-one seats, garlanded, with a shrine on the dash and a driver who had been doing that road for twenty-two years and treated the queue as a scheduled social occasion.

A family car with a mattress on the roof and a plate from a prefecture three hundred kilometres east, westbound, with three children in the back and a great deal in the boot, whose father stood outside it for two hours looking at nothing.

Two Ministry vehicles, black plates, parked crosswise at the head of everything.

The livestock truck, with a plate nobody could identify, forty goats, and a smell that made Ciaccona reconsider her entire position on stinky tofu.

And the *Lucky Bird*: an apricot-and-tooth-yellow decommissioned mobile dental surgery with a Rabelle transit plate, five black characters and a chicken shop's name on the cab door, four water drums on the roof, two bikes on the frame, and a four-metre ceramic angel kneeling on the sample bed under a tarp.

Four separate people came and looked at it in the first hour.

---

Queue culture asserted itself inside forty minutes, which is fast, and it asserted itself because Mei got out of the truck at minute six and went to talk to the goats.

By minute forty she had: the livestock driver's name, his route, his opinion of the Ministry's new inspection regime, the fact that the alternate convoy timing had changed in the fourth month, and a bag of dried apricots she had been given and had immediately given away.

By minute ninety she was on the pilgrimage bus.

<font color=teal>"She's on the bus."</font>

<font color=royalblue>"Nineteen minutes."</font>

<font color=teal>"There are forty-one pilgrims on that bus and she has four sentences of the language."</font>

<font color=royalblue>"Mm."</font>

<font color=teal>"How is she *doing* that? She's got four sentences. She has four sentences and one of them summons a doctor."</font>

<font color=royalblue>"She has four sentences, a face that people confess to, and a total absence of embarrassment,"</font> said Iuno, watching a bus through a windscreen with an expression she was making no attempt whatever to manage. <font color=royalblue>"I have watched her walk a hooded fugitive through two armed checkpoints by explaining that the woman was growing mushrooms. Twice. It worked *both times.*"</font>

<font color=teal>"...I want that written down."</font>

<font color=royalblue>"It is written down. It is written down in *four places* and one of them is a court register."</font>

<font color=royalblue>"That is a *vehicle log.*"</font>

<font color=moccasin>"It's a vehicle document about the vehicle."</font>

<font color=royalblue>"It is a *crayon.*"</font>

<font color=royalblue>"...Entered as an annexe,"</font> she said.

## KELVET — 9TH IN LINE

## KELVET — VEHICLE 1

They crossed at twenty past six, at first light, first in the alternate convoy, on a wet road with the mountains behind them going pink from the top down.

The barrier was a red-and-white pole on a counterweight, and a man raised it by hand.

That was all it was.

<font color=skyblue>"Is that it?"</font>

<font color=teal>"That's it."</font>

<font color=skyblue>"That's a *pole.*"</font>

<font color=teal>"It weighs about nine kilos. It has cost us fourteen hours, a seal, a bond, a personally liable officer, two spoiled forms, four pots of a young man's tea and roughly nine years off my life, and it goes up when a man leans on the other end."</font>

<font color=indianred>"Every border's a pole."</font>

<font color=moccasin>"Some of them are a *rope.*"</font>

<font color=indianred>"Mm."</font>

---

Ruan Zhi came out onto the step as the *Lucky Bird* pulled forward, with a cup in his hand, in the cold, without a coat on, which Xie noticed and wrote down nowhere.

He watched it go under the pole.

Then he said something to nobody, in his own language, at conversational volume, which Cartethyia heard through an open window at eleven metres and kept entirely to herself, then and afterward, on the grounds that it had been said to the air and to nobody in that truck.

She wrote it down in the folder, phonetically, on the inside of the back cover, and asked a letter-writer at a gate in Jinzhou what it meant eleven days later, and paid her by the character, and cried in the street about it for approximately forty seconds, and then went and bought lemon cakes.

---

The road came down.

It came down for two hours through cloud, and out of the cloud at nine hundred metres into a morning with the sun on it, and below them, forty kilometres out and three thousand metres down, in a bowl of water with a dam above it, with the lamp-grid still burning on the north side because the light had reached the east bank first and the switching was done by hand, there was a city.

The willows on the west bank were three days ahead of the ones on the east.

The letter had said so. They could see it from here.
