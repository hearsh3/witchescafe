# THE GRAND PLAN
### Papers of Iuno Caecilia Severa, High Priestess of Septimont
*Recovered from the eastern tower of the Tetragon Temple. Thirty years of material. Catalogued in the order the author kept them, which was chronological until it stopped being anything.*

*— R. Marigold, Temple caretaker*

---

**ITEM 1.** *Index card, temple stock, yellowed. The handwriting is a child's, copying an adult's dictation. Pinned above the desk for thirty years; the pinhole has worn through.*

> A PRIESTESS MUST NEVER FALL IN LOVE.
>
> Love clouds the water. The water is the work.
> — Mother's rule. First rule.

*Beneath, in the same child's hand, a diligent annotation:*

> Understood.

---

**ITEM 2.** *Field assessment, loose leaf, water-ringed. Age seventeen.*

SUBJECT: female, approx. 19. Canals, third district. Homeless. Furious.
RESONANCE: magnetic. Ten-meter field. Unremarkable by every metric.
WILL: off the scale I was taught to use.

Probability of Ephorate, unguided: 4%.
Probability of Ephorate, guided: 62%.

Guidance begins tomorrow. Supply lines first. Senators after.

*Margin, decades later, softer ink:*

> *Revised: 71%. Revised: 88%. Revised: she did it herself, I just held the map.*
> *She likes apples. Irrelevant. Filed anyway.*

---

**ITEM 3.** *Prophecy index card, red border — the Temple's marking for a sealed reading. The card has been handled so often the border has gone pink.*

READING 0-0-1. THE APPOINTED HOUR.

The tide comes for Septimont on a fixed date. I have seen the date. I have seen the water in the streets and the Colosseum full of it.

The tide accepts one life, given at the precipice, at the hour.

One life buys the city.

The prophecy names the life. It is mine.

*The last line has been struck through once, thinly, and rewritten below in firmer hand:*

> The prophecy names **a** life.

---

**ITEM 4.** *Ledger page, ruled, kept over many years in many inks. The heading is formal. The totals are exact.*

CHOSEN FUTURES — CASUALTY RECORD

Hunt of the 3rd year: 12 riders committed. 9 returned. Alternative futures: 4 returned / 6 returned / 9. Chose 9.
Hunt of the 7th year: 31 committed. 26 returned. Chose 26.
Hunt of the 11th year: ...

*The column continues down the page and onto the reverse. At the bottom, a running total, updated in increments:*

> 173.
>
> I always choose the future where the most live. Fewest. Never none.

*Margin, small:*

> *I watched each of them mount their gryphons. Every one of them thanked me for the blessing.*

---

**ITEM 5.** *File jacket, twenty years old. The label reads* CONTENDER MYA — DISPOSITION. *Inside: one page. Six lines have been struck out with ink so heavy the nib tore the paper. What remains:*

Body recovered by J. Silva. Transferred per agreement.
Payment: silence regarding [struck] and the Whispers.
Consideration received: the city stands.

To survive the tide, one must make the right choices.

*Margin, in a hand ten years younger than the file:*

> *I don't want this.*

*Margin, in a hand ten years older:*

> *Please, someone stop me.*

---

**ITEM 6.** *Memorandum, formal draft with revisions. Titled* SUBSTITUTE — SELECTION CRITERIA.

The tide takes a protagonist. It has done so twice: the saint, the champion. It prefers a center. Therefore build one.

Requirements:
1. Resonance capacity, upper percentile. The hour will demand it.
2. Zero ties. Grief propagates through ties. Minimize propagation.
3. Zero history. History resists authorship.
4. Trainable. Eleven months minimum.
5. Willing. *(Struck through.)* Guidable. *(Struck through.)* Bound.

An amnesiac satisfies 2 through 5 simultaneously.

The sea occasionally provides.

*Margin, undated:*

> *Reading the tides for a body I could point at. This is the work now. The water is the work.*

---

**ITEM 7.** *Retrieval log, single sheet, salt-stiffened at one corner.*

Dawn. The shore below the eastern cliffs, as foreseen.
Subject: female. Adult. Tacet Mark active, dormant profile extraordinary. Memory: absent. Confirmed by direct question, three phrasings.

She asked why I saved her. Told her the truth: I'm selfish.

Designation required for handling.

> *Assigned: MEI.*
> *"It's Mei, okay? You're called Mei. Now keep up."*
> *She kept up.*

---

**ITEM 8.** *Training schedule, gridded. Two columns: OBJECTIVE / OUTCOME. The objective column is typed. The outcome column deteriorates.*

WEEK 3 — Resonance Wings. Cliff instruction.
OBJECTIVE: functional flight, combat baseline.
OUTCOME: achieved, day two.

*Margin:*

> *She laughed the whole way down. Both times. Unnecessary data. Recorded anyway.*

WEEK 9 — Blade forms, counter-tempo.
OBJECTIVE: survivability, plateau engagements.
OUTCOME: achieved. She coils before she strikes. Log the tell.

*Margin:*

> *Logged the tell. Sat with the log for an hour. Filed it under WEAKNESSES and felt the word in my teeth.*

---

**ITEM 9.** *Protocol sheet, headed* BINDING — SCHEDULE OF CONTACT. *The most annotated page in the collection.*

Mechanism: ritual of two cups. Jasmine (subject), earl grey (mine). Fixed location, variable arrival. Arrive late by design. Cold tea teaches waiting. Waiting binds.

Estimated bond strength required by the hour: total.
Estimated bond strength, week 14: sufficient.
Estimated bond strength, week 20: [blank]

*Margins, accumulating down the side of the page and onto the back:*

> *She waited three hours today. The tea went cold. I drank it anyway.*
> *She said, make it hot next time. I said I would. WHY DID I SAY THAT. Correction is scheduled lateness. The lateness is the mechanism.*
> *Subject ordered a second cup without asking. Unusual. She has begun assuming I will come.*
> *I have begun coming.*

---

**ITEM 10.** *Loose observation slips, various stock — the backs of requisition forms, a torn concert programme, one napkin. Bundled with string. A sample:*

> *Subject taught herself three card tricks between yesterday and today. Unaccounted-for time. Purpose: unknown. She performed them for the harbor children. Purpose: known.*

> *Fisalia Manor. Entered via window. Fed subject. Stated aloud: this means nothing. Assessment: liar.*

> *Masked promenade. Tucked a strand of hair behind her ear. Cost: nothing, I told myself. Invoice pending.*

> *She flushes when I claim her. "I keep what's mine, Rover." Filed under LEVERAGE.*
> *Refiled.*

---

**ITEM 11.** *Operational directive, Septimont period, typed with force. Titled* ESTRANGEMENT PROTOCOL.

Rationale: at the hour, attachment to the officiant compromises the subject's function. Distance is optimal. Hatred is acceptable. Hatred is fuel — it keeps a fighter swinging long enough to be spent.

Implementation: reveal office publicly. Withhold explanation. Let the city's rumors do the labor.

STATUS, DAY 1: implemented. Subject observed at the arena, avoiding my eye.
ASSESSMENT: successful.

*The word "successful" has been circled, then annotated:*

> *That's amputation, and you performed it on yourself.*

*Below, in cramped lines running sideways up the margin:*

> *Followed her home tonight. Rooftop by rooftop. Logged as reconnaissance.*
> *Stood at her window in the rain. Hand raised. Withdrew it.*
> *Knuckles: split. Stone: fine. Discrepancy noted.*

---

**ITEM 12.** *Basin transcript — the Temple's format for recording a scried vision. The page is torn diagonally and has been taped back together, imperfectly, so the lines misalign.*

VISION, APPOINTED HOUR MINUS 15.

Black sand. The tide standing open. Subject at the center, as designed.
Subject turns to the officiant.
Subject's expression: [struck]
Subject mouths: *it's okay.*

*Everything below the tear is written after the taping:*

> *She forgives me. In the water, at the end, she forgives me.*
> *The plan requires her rage and she brings me absolution instead.*
> *I broke the basin. The Temple has other basins. That is the problem with the Temple.*

---

**ITEM 13.** *Countdown sheets, one per day, numbered 15 through 5. Early sheets carry full operational summaries. The summaries shrink. Sheet 11 reads, in full:*

> Eleven. She named the team today. Wuthering Waves. She was smiling when she wrote it.

*Sheet 8:*

> Eight. Tavern, midnight. Two cups again. She threatened to leave. I told her she wouldn't. My predictions have never once been wrong. I have started hating that sentence.

*Sheet 6:*

> Six. Augusta dressed me this morning. Slapped me. "You read fate. You read your heart. Which can you control?"
> Three of my own voices answered her. They said: *you can defy fate.* I have scheduled their silencing.
> The silencing failed.

*Sheet 5:*

> Five. She asked me to smile. What came out was a wound.
> She called these months a nice reverie and thanked me for them.
> Thanked. Me.

---

**ITEM 14.** *Official prophecy, formal calligraphy, Temple seal — the copy issued to the archive. The reading concerns crop tariffs. The margin concerns something else. This is one of many; the caretaker attests there are twenty years of these, and that she read every one.*

> *I don't want this. Please, someone stop me.*

---

**ITEM 15.** *The final page. Heavy stock. Drafting ink. The Grand Plan, complete form — the plot board reduced to a single diagram. Thirty years of threads: the Ephorate, the Silvas, the bargain, the Agon, the hunts, the 173, the tea, the cliff, the wings. Every line runs inward. Every line converges on a single point at the center of the page, boxed, labeled:*

# MEI

*Below the diagram, the disposition table, in the author's own hand:*

| ASSET | DISPOSITION |
|---|---|
| Septimont | preserved |
| Augusta | preserved |
| The Temple | preserved |
| Mei | expendable |

*The table is untouched. The ink of the table has aged.*

*In the margin, beside her name, in slightly different ink — newer, blacker, the pen pressed hard enough to emboss the reverse of the page — two words, written and left standing:*

> **I can't.**

---

*Catalogue note: the collection ends here. The countdown sheets numbered 4 through 1 were never written. The final page was found separate from the rest, unfiled, in the drawer where the author kept her mother's index card. The two were together.*

*Filed under: unfinished.*

*— R.M.*
