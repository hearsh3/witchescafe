## THE THIRD SEAT

Narrator: The corner by the window, under the shelf with the stitched cloth bird on it, has had a seat kept at it every week for a year.

Yangyang set it before anybody sat down. Bowl, cup, chopsticks, the good spoon. She did it early and quickly and out of the flow of traffic, the way she has done it fifty-odd times, and then went and did something else.

Jinhsi saw her do it.

Jinhsi wrote the ruling. Her seal is on the drawer the report went into. She has read the word 接風 in three separate hands this season and has argued about it with a Ministry in writing.

She sat four feet from the seat all evening and said nothing about it whatsoever.

At about half nine, mid-conversation, Cartethyia reached across and put a spoonful of the bitter melon into the third bowl.

<font color=skyblue>"She likes this one."</font>

<font color=teal>"You've decided that."</font>

<font color=skyblue>"She *does*."</font>

<font color=teal>"Based on."</font>

<font color=skyblue>"Based on the *steam*, Cia, look at the — "</font>

<font color=teal>"The steam does that with everything."</font>

<font color=skyblue>"The steam does it MORE with this."</font>

The argument ran a while. Amy took a position. Chixia took the opposite position out of pure sport. Baizhi produced a devastating remark about the draught from the door hinge which was ignored by everybody.

And about four minutes into it, without any particular ceremony, Jinhsi picked up the dish of bitter melon and passed it down the table toward the empty seat, the way you pass a dish, because it had come to her and the empty place was the next place along.

Yangyang saw it.

Yangyang picked up her cup and drank out of it and put it down and did not say anything, and her ears went pink under the hat, and the argument about the steam went on for another six minutes and was never resolved.

## THE LATE GUEST

Narrator: She came in at ten with road dust on her boots and her coat over her arm.

Nobody in the front room recognised her. Nobody in the back room recognised her either, except two.

Iuno's cup stopped halfway.

Lupa's ears went round.

Jinhsi's face did something that nobody at that table had seen it do all night, and it was gone in about a second and a half.

<font color=gold>"…You are back early."</font>

<font color=orangered>"I finished the second column."</font>

<font color=gold>"You said three days."</font>

<font color=orangered>"I said three days on Saturday. It is Tuesday and the strongbox is in the cart and Wen has the receipts." </font>Changli stood in the doorway of a back room with sixteen people in it and looked at the table for slightly longer than a person normally looks at a table.

Then Mrs Ren said, <font color=darkkhaki>"Shut the door, it's coming in on my hip,"</font> and that was the end of the doorway.

Narrator: She was absorbed in under a minute.

Cartethyia took her coat and put a paper person in her hand. Somebody found a rice sack. Chixia, who had never seen her before in her life, put the crispy thing platter in front of her and told her at some length that there was extra and that she was a generous person. Little Guo brought a bowl without being asked, having reached the stage of the evening where he had stopped being frightened of anybody.

<font color=mistyrose>"Right. You. Mangosteens."</font>

<font color=orangered>"…I beg your pardon."</font>

<font color=mistyrose>"Do you work with her?"</font> Amy jerked a thumb at Jinhsi. <font color=mistyrose>"You've got the face. Everyone in that building's got the face."</font>

<font color=orangered>"I work with her."</font>

<font color=mistyrose>"Then settle something. The mangosteen. Was it a code."</font>

<font color=orangered>"…"</font>

<font color=gold>"It was a mangosteen."</font>

<font color=mistyrose>"See, she keeps *saying* that."</font>

<font color=orangered>"It was a mangosteen. They are in season eleven days. She had me put four crates of them through the ward offices in the second week of the season for six years running and there is a line in the prefectural accounts about it that has been queried at audit twice."</font>

<font color=mistyrose>"…Four *crates*."</font>

<font color=gold>"The audit query is unresolved."</font>

<font color=orangered>"The audit query will remain unresolved."</font>

Amy sat back on her crate and looked at the two of them for a moment with an expression that Mei, twenty years from now, would still be able to describe exactly.

<font color=mistyrose>"Okay. Fine. It was a mangosteen."</font>

<font color=skyblue>"I *said* it was a mangosteen."</font>

<font color=mistyrose>"You said it was a bomb."</font>

<font color=skyblue>"On the first day! On the first day everything's a bomb!"</font>

She got the goose diagram explained to her by Chixia, who still did not know who she was, and who was still insisting she would not be participating.

She looked at it seriously.

Narrator: Iuno had been watching the room.

Not the way she used to watch rooms. She had a bowl and a ledger and a woman with a bad hip arguing at her elbow, and she was watching about a third of it, out of habit she has stopped apologising for.

So she was the only one who saw it.

Changli, on a rice sack, in a back room, with a paper person in her coat pocket and somebody else's chopsticks, stopped eating for about four seconds and looked down the table at a girl of nineteen who had sauce on her cuff and was being shouted at about a pickle by a woman with a cane.

That was all it was. Four seconds, and then somebody passed her the vinegar and she said thank you by name.

Iuno went back to column four.

<font color=darkkhaki>"You've stopped."</font>

<font color=royalblue>"I have not stopped."</font>

<font color=darkkhaki>"Your pen's stopped."</font>

<font color=royalblue>"…Column six."</font>

<font color=darkkhaki>"*Four.*"</font>

## WHAT PANHUA WASHED

Narrator: Panhua sat down twice.

The first time was at about a quarter to ten. She sat at the end nearest the hatch with a bowl she had made for herself out of the ends of four things and ate it in about ninety seconds while Zhao explained a gearbox to her, and got up.

The second time was near eleven, when the front was closed and the last of the step had come in and the room had gone from loud to the other thing, and she sat down properly with a cup and did not get up for twenty minutes.

Ciaccona was in the hatch by then, asking questions.

At some point Panhua said one sentence to the Magistrate of Jinzhou, which is one more than she had said all evening.

<font color=peachpuff>"You eat too fast."</font>

<font color=gold>"…I am told."</font>

<font color=peachpuff>"Come at four in the afternoon. Nobody's in. It's quiet and the oil's fresh."</font>

<font color=gold>"…"</font>

<font color=peachpuff>"Mm."</font>

And that was that, and Panhua got up and started stacking.

Chixia, who was three feet away and had heard all of it, looked at the ceiling and made a small noise.

---

Narrator: They went out at about half eleven.

The lane was cool. The lamps had been down to the low ones for an hour. The rain had gone off toward the plain and taken the smell of the cut fields somewhere else.

Changli got to the door first with her coat over her arm and waited there while the room emptied past her, and Iuno came out last because she had been putting Mrs Ren's ledger back into Mrs Ren's bag with the pages the right way round.

They ended up in the doorway together for about six seconds.

<font color=orangered>"Tomorrow."</font>

<font color=royalblue>"…"</font>

<font color=orangered>"You asked when. Tomorrow."</font>

<font color=royalblue>"Do you want somebody in the room."</font>

<font color=orangered>"No."</font>

<font color=royalblue>"Good answer."</font>

Changli went up the lane after the Magistrate of Jinzhou, who was walking home on foot with four escorts badly hidden and a paper person in her coat pocket and sauce on her cuff, and who stopped at the corner to look at something in a shop window that had been closed for six hours.

Lupa came out of the door behind Iuno and stood there.

<font color=indianred>"You've got the face on."</font>

<font color=royalblue>"I am aware."</font>

<font color=indianred>"Is it tomorrow yet."</font>

<font color=royalblue>"…No."</font>

<font color=indianred>"Then walk."</font>

They walked.

Narrator: Yangyang did the count on the step with the lamp over the door and the ledger on her knee, because there was nowhere flat left indoors.

Mei sat down next to her to wait.

<font color=moccasin>"I said fourteen."</font>

<font color=yellowgreen>"You were wrong."</font>

<font color=moccasin>"By how much."</font>

<font color=yellowgreen>"Panhua washed nineteen bowls."</font>

<font color=moccasin>"That's not people, that's bowls."</font>

<font color=yellowgreen>"It is how she counts."</font> The pen moved. <font color=yellowgreen>"I have seventeen at the table, four in the lane who were fed and are not on any list, one who came at ten, one who left at eight and told Cartethyia about barges, and the seat."</font>

<font color=moccasin>"So what's the number."</font>

Yangyang looked at it for a while.

<font color=yellowgreen>"…The number does not come out."</font>

<font color=moccasin>"Yeah."</font>

<font color=yellowgreen>"It has never once come out at this house."</font>

<font color=moccasin>"Write down that it doesn't come out."</font>

Yangyang wrote down that it did not come out.

Then she wrote three more words underneath it, in the second ink, and boxed them, and shut the book before Mei could look, and stood up and said the fish had been good.

Behind them, in the empty back room, Panhua turned the lamps down one at a time — the long table, the window, the shelf with the stitched cloth bird on it — and left the one at the back left by the door hinge, as she does, because it is on the hinge side and gets knocked, and it is easier to leave it.

It burned by itself for a while in an empty room.

Then it leaned once, over toward the corner and the third seat, and steadied, and went out on its own about a minute later, which is roughly a minute longer than that lamp has ever managed.

Panhua, from the kitchen, without turning round:

<font color=peachpuff>"Goodnight."</font>
