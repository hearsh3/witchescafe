# Rogatio

### 舊事 — OLD BUSINESS

*Mrs Ren's guesthouse, above the east market. The sixth month. The twenty-sixth day in the country, on a ninety-day stamp, in a room with a window that will not latch.*

---

## THE TIN

There are eleven cups of tea left in the world and they are in a tin.

The tin is black and gilt and dented on the lower third from a fall it took in Bailment, and it was bought in the dock market at Ragunna in the second month of a year that has since been called four different things, and it has crossed one sea, one glacier, and eleven hundred kilometres of road that Chisa still refers to, without adjectives, as *the road*. Nobody in Jinzhou sells earl grey. Iuno has asked in nine shops. The ninth shopkeeper produced, with enormous kindness, a jasmine so good that Iuno bought two ounces of it and has never once opened the paper.

Mei made a cup out of the tin at half past ten, because Iuno had come in at half past ten with a ledger under her arm and a face on, and Mei's response to a face is a kettle.

Then Amy came through with a cable fault and a theory, and the theory took forty minutes, and by the time the room emptied the cup was standing on the washstand at the temperature of the room.

Iuno picked it up and drank it.

<font color=moccasin>"Oh — no, hang on, that's — I'll do another one — "</font>

<font color=royalblue>"Sit down."</font>

<font color=moccasin>"It's *stone* cold, Iuiu, there's — I can see the *skin* on it — "</font>

<font color=royalblue>"Mei."</font>

Mei sat down on the end of the bed, which is four feet wide and hers by a vote of six to nil with one abstention, and watched the woman she lives with drink cold tea standing up at a washstand in a rented room.

Iuno finished it. She set the cup down and turned it a little on the wood, by the rim, which is a thing she picked up off somebody and has never once acknowledged picking up.

<font color=royalblue>"I have nine questions,"</font> she said.

## NINE

<font color=moccasin>"…Now?"</font>

<font color=royalblue>"Now."</font>

<font color=moccasin>"It's — "</font> Mei looked at the window, which told her nothing, and then at the lane, which told her the noodle place had shut. <font color=moccasin>"It's late."</font>

<font color=royalblue>"It is one in the morning and there is weather coming at about five, which I know because my elbow has been announcing it since the second hour, and I have had these nine questions for two years and I have declined to ask them on something in the region of four hundred occasions."</font> Iuno pulled the chair out from under the coat and sat in it, which meant moving the coat, which she did with one hand and without looking at it. <font color=royalblue>"Tonight I drank your tea cold in a country where nobody has ever heard of me, and the argument ran out."</font>

<font color=moccasin>"What argument?"</font>

<font color=royalblue>"That you would tell me when you wanted to."</font>

Mei thought about that.

<font color=moccasin>"That's a good argument."</font>

<font color=royalblue>"It is an excellent argument. It is also precisely how I got through thirty years in a building without once asking anybody anything I actually wanted to know."</font> Iuno put her hand flat on her knee. <font color=royalblue>"I would like to establish the terms."</font>

<font color=moccasin>"Course you would."</font>

<font color=royalblue>"I know the answers."</font>

<font color=moccasin>"…Right."</font>

<font color=royalblue>"I want that on the table before we start, because you will otherwise spend an hour trying to work out what I am fishing for and you will perform, and I have had eleven years of people performing at me and I have developed an allergy."</font> She turned the cup another degree. <font color=royalblue>"I have the outside of every one of these. I have angles, and reports, and the position of the sun, and for most of them I have four futures I read at the time and can still recite. I have never once had the inside. I have never had a single one of them from the person it happened to."</font>

Mei was quiet.

<font color=royalblue>"So they are not hard questions,"</font> Iuno said. <font color=royalblue>"They are old ones. And I am asking them badly, at one in the morning, on a Friday, because I have run out of ways of asking them well."</font>

<font color=moccasin>"Did you write them down?"</font>

<font color=royalblue>"No."</font>

<font color=moccasin>"You write *everything* down. You've got a page for my *bruises* — "</font>

<font color=royalblue>"I have four pages for your bruises and I intend to have forty."</font> Iuno's voice did not move. <font color=royalblue>"I did not write these down because writing them down would have made them a file, and I decided in the frostlands that I was finished making files about you."</font>

The lane did one of its four sounds.

<font color=moccasin>"…So where've you been keeping them?"</font>

*In here,* said the High Priestess, from the velvet lounge in the small warm room behind the sternum. *Numbered. Alphabetised as a bonus. You are welcome, darling.*

<font color=royalblue>"They have been in storage,"</font> Iuno said.

<font color=moccasin>"That's a *yes*, that's — Iuiu, that's you saying *her*, you've gone all — "</font>

<font color=royalblue>"One,"</font> said Iuno.

## ONE. THE MUSHROOMS.

<font color=royalblue>"Whisperwind Haven. The checkpoint on the landward side. A grey cloak, a sprig of somebody's rosemary, and a hooded assistant who would not lower her hood."</font>

Mei's whole face came up.

<font color=moccasin>"Oh, that's a *good* one — "</font>

<font color=royalblue>"You told two armed men that your companion was fungating."</font>

<font color=moccasin>"She *was*."</font>

<font color=royalblue>"She was the Blessed Maiden of Rinascita with fifty thousand shells on her head."</font>

<font color=moccasin>"Yeah, and she was *fungating*, that's — those aren't — you can be both — "</font>

Iuno waited. She has an excellent wait. It is the same wait she used on a Senate of sixteen and it works better on Mei than it ever did on them, which she finds insulting.

Mei got to the end of the laugh and scratched the back of her neck.

<font color=moccasin>"It's a good word,"</font> she said.

<font color=royalblue>"Mm."</font>

<font color=moccasin>"No, it — you can't argue with it. That's the thing about it. You say *she's ill* and they want a name for it. You say *fungating* and they've got a name for it and it's worse than anything they had, and their whole face closes like a shop."</font>

<font color=royalblue>"You had practised it."</font>

<font color=moccasin>"Three days on the road."</font>

<font color=royalblue>"Out loud?"</font>

<font color=moccasin>"To a *hedge*."</font>

Iuno put her hand over her eyes for a moment.

<font color=moccasin>"You've got to say it *sad*,"</font> Mei said, warming up. <font color=moccasin>"That's the whole technique. If you say it like a joke they arrest you. You've got to say it like you're carrying it. Like it's the fourth thing that's gone wrong this month and you're just — getting on."</font>

<font color=royalblue>"And it worked twice."</font>

<font color=moccasin>"Landward gate and the Montelli one. Same man on the second, that's the — that's what I keep thinking about."</font>

<font color=royalblue>"Why?"</font>

<font color=moccasin>"Because the second lot had *heard about the first lot* — "</font>

<font color=royalblue>"Why did you do it,"</font> said Iuno.

Mei stopped.

The lamp did what lamps do.

<font color=moccasin>"…She hadn't said anything for two hours,"</font> Mei said.

<font color=royalblue>"Go on."</font>

<font color=moccasin>"We'd come off the windmill and she'd had that thing in her hands, the — the print, the one with the horse on it, and she'd been *fine*, she'd been doing the voice, and then we got on the road and about a mile out she went quiet and she stayed quiet, and Ciaccona tried her twice and I let it go, and by the checkpoint we were at two hours."</font> Mei was looking at the floorboards. <font color=moccasin>"You can't get somebody out of that by asking. I've tried. Asking just adds a thing to the pile."</font>

<font color=royalblue>"So."</font>

<font color=moccasin>"So I said something so stupid she'd have to have an *opinion* on it."</font>

<font color=royalblue>"…"</font>

<font color=moccasin>"And under the hood her shoulders went."</font> Mei did it, badly, a small jerk of both shoulders. <font color=moccasin>"That's it. That's the whole thing. I got a shoulder out of her at a checkpoint and then she was talking again by the gate and she called me a *lunatic* in front of a customs man and I've never been so pleased in my life."</font>

<font color=royalblue>"You were performing to an audience of one."</font>

<font color=moccasin>"Yeah."</font>

<font color=royalblue>"Two,"</font> said Iuno.

Mei looked up.

<font color=royalblue>"There is a customs roof,"</font> Iuno said, with dignity.

<font color=moccasin>"There's a — "</font>

<font color=royalblue>"There is a customs roof at Whisperwind Haven and it has a very good sightline and I was on it, and I had been on it since the eleventh hour, and I had a bow, and I heard a woman say the word *fungating* to a state official with her hand on her heart."</font> Iuno's chin was extremely level. <font color=royalblue>"I had to leave the position."</font>

<font color=moccasin>"You *left the roof.*"</font>

<font color=royalblue>"I withdrew to a secondary vantage."</font>

<font color=moccasin>"You LEFT THE ROOF because you were LAUGHING — "</font>

<font color=royalblue>"I relocated for operational reasons — "</font>

<font color=moccasin>"IUIU — "</font>

<font color=indianred>"*It is one in the morning,*"</font> observed the stairhead, through a floor.

They both went quiet with their hands over their mouths like two people who have been caught, which is what they were, and the house settled, and Mei fell over sideways onto the bed with her fist against her teeth and shook for about forty seconds.

## TWO. THE ARROW.

<font color=royalblue>"The canal district. Lorelei."</font>

<font color=moccasin>"Mm."</font>

<font color=royalblue>"Did you know it was mine."</font>

Mei turned her head on the quilt.

<font color=moccasin>"I knew it was *someone's*."</font>

<font color=royalblue>"How."</font>

<font color=moccasin>"Because it came in flat and it came in *early*."</font> Mei put her arm behind her head. <font color=moccasin>"Nobody who's just arrived shoots like that. That was somebody who'd been watching the fight for a while and had picked the moment about four moves back."</font>

<font color=royalblue>"That is a very good reading."</font>

<font color=moccasin>"I'd had a lot of very interesting weeks by then."</font>

Iuno turned the cup on the washstand.

<font color=royalblue>"You said something afterwards."</font>

<font color=moccasin>"…Did I?"</font>

<font color=royalblue>"You stood up in about four inches of canal water with a mad Remnant twenty metres away and a priestess screaming at you to move, and you looked at the roofline, and you said two words to a chimney."</font>

Mei's face did something complicated and delighted.

<font color=moccasin>"I did do that."</font>

<font color=royalblue>"You did do that."</font>

<font color=moccasin>"I said *thanks, mate*."</font>

<font color=royalblue>"You said *thank you*. I read it off your mouth."</font>

<font color=moccasin>"I said *thanks, mate*, and then I said thank you *properly* because I thought whoever it was might be posh."</font>

<font color=royalblue>"…"</font>

<font color=moccasin>"Which, in fairness."</font>

Iuno let that sit where it was.

<font color=royalblue>"You were looking at the wrong roof,"</font> she said. <font color=royalblue>"I have carried that for two years. Somebody put an arrow through a mask for you and you thanked a chimney belonging to a spice warehouse."</font>

<font color=moccasin>"No, that was the right chimney."</font>

<font color=royalblue>"I was three roofs east."</font>

Mei sat up on one elbow.

<font color=moccasin>"…Huh."</font>

Neither of them said anything for four or five seconds. The curtain, which had been doing nothing at all, went on doing nothing at all with what had begun over the last three weeks to feel like effort.

<font color=royalblue>"Anyway,"</font> Iuno said.

<font color=moccasin>"Yeah."</font>

<font color=royalblue>"That was not the question."</font>

<font color=moccasin>"Go on then."</font>

Iuno looked at her own hand for a while. Then she said, in the flat voice she uses for bad news because she was told once, in a truck, on a bad night, to stop making it softer:

<font color=royalblue>"I was not supposed to shoot."</font>

<font color=moccasin>"I know."</font>

<font color=royalblue>"You do not know the extent of it. That arrow was outside every parameter I had set for myself. I had a document. There was a document, Mei, with headings, in which I had established the conditions under which I would intervene, and the whole architecture of it depended on your capacity to survive things I did not touch."</font> The one hand flexed. <font color=royalblue>"I spent two days afterwards writing a justification for it. Four pages. Nobody has ever read them and nobody ever will, because I burned them at Porto-Veno with a candle I had to ask a servant for, which was humiliating."</font>

<font color=moccasin>"Four pages."</font>

<font color=royalblue>"Four pages of tactical necessity."</font>

<font color=moccasin>"And what was the actual one?"</font>

<font color=royalblue>"One line."</font>

<font color=moccasin>"Did you write it?"</font>

<font color=royalblue>"No."</font>

Mei waited, which is a thing she has learned to do and which took her considerably longer than the sword.

<font color=royalblue>"You were going to lose the hand,"</font> Iuno said.

Mei looked down at her left hand, lying on the quilt, doing nothing.

<font color=royalblue>"You had it flat on the flagstone taking your weight and her second lance was already coming round on the low line, and I could see the whole of it, and I could see the four seconds after it, and in all four of them you get up."</font> Iuno's voice was completely level, which is how she does the ones that cost. <font color=royalblue>"You get up with your hand off at the wrist and you get Phoebe out of the water and you make a joke about it inside the hour. I watched that happen four times in about a second and a half, and then I discovered that I had already shot."</font>

The lane made a small sound.

Mei flexed the hand once, and then again, and looked at it like a woman checking a receipt.

<font color=moccasin>"Thanks,"</font> she said.

<font color=royalblue>"…"</font>

<font color=moccasin>"Two years late. Still counts."</font>

<font color=royalblue>"That is what you said to the chimney."</font>

<font color=moccasin>"Yeah, well. It's a good line, it's in circulation."</font>

## THREE. THE HORN.

Iuno got up and moved the lamp to the sill, where the light is worse and the room is easier, which is a thing she does when she is about to spend something.

<font color=royalblue>"Avinoleum,"</font> she said. <font color=royalblue>"The last chamber. After."</font>

Mei's face went carefully ordinary.

<font color=royalblue>"You asked a god to give her back and it named a price, and the price was a bound fate, and you took it."</font> Iuno stayed at the window. <font color=royalblue>"Cantarella's account says *without hesitating*. Cartethyia's account says *instantly*. Ciaccona had it third-hand and put it in a song, and in the song it is *before the words were out*, because it scans."</font>

<font color=moccasin>"It's a good song."</font>

<font color=royalblue>"It is a very good song and I have loathed it for two years. How long did it take you."</font>

<font color=moccasin>"Didn't take any time."</font>

<font color=royalblue>"That is the answer I have had from four people and I have never once believed it."</font>

Mei sat with her forearms on her knees.

<font color=moccasin>"…Four minutes,"</font> she said.

Iuno turned round.

<font color=moccasin>"Cantarella was talking at it. She talked at it for about four minutes — she was doing the thing, the *poisoner* thing, where she takes a god apart with grammar — and I was stood behind her going through what I had."</font> Mei rubbed her thumb across her palm. <font color=moccasin>"Which was: a body that knits. A name somebody handed me on a beach. And nobody anywhere waiting up."</font>

<font color=royalblue>"…"</font>

<font color=moccasin>"So by the time it turned round and asked, I'd already done it. I had the number in my mouth. That's why it looked fast."</font> She shrugged. <font color=moccasin>"I do sums all the time. I'm just rubbish at showing my working."</font>

Iuno stood at the window with her back to the lane and did not say anything for a while.

<font color=royalblue>"You costed yourself."</font>

<font color=moccasin>"Everybody costs everything. Everybody. All the time. I just did it in four minutes instead of over a life, and I got a cheap number, and that made the rest of it easy."</font>

<font color=royalblue>"And that is the part you think is normal."</font>

<font color=moccasin>"Iuiu — "</font>

<font color=royalblue>"I am not doing a face. I am registering that you have just told me the cheapest thing in the room was you, in the tone of a woman describing a bus timetable."</font>

<font color=moccasin>"Well, it *was*."</font>

<font color=royalblue>"Mm."</font>

Mei let out a breath.

<font color=moccasin>"You want the other bit."</font>

<font color=royalblue>"I want the other bit."</font>

<font color=moccasin>"The other bit's that I was *furious*."</font>

Iuno came away from the window and sat back down in the chair.

<font color=moccasin>"She'd done the sums right,"</font> Mei said. <font color=moccasin>"That's what got me. A girl sits in a tower for ten years doing long division on herself and gets it *right*, and everybody in that room could check her working, and the answer was *you should stop*. And she did it. And she was *calm* about it, she gave her whole self away in about eleven words like she was passing the salt."</font>

<font color=royalblue>"*Take my power, my blessings, my past.*"</font>

<font color=moccasin>"You've got it word for word."</font>

<font color=royalblue>"I have had it word for word for two years. Say the rest."</font>

<font color=moccasin>"There isn't a — "</font>

<font color=royalblue>"There is at least a paragraph."</font>

Mei laughed at the floor.

<font color=moccasin>"The rest is that I couldn't argue with the sum,"</font> she said. <font color=moccasin>"I stood there and I went through it and it *held*. So the only thing left was to break it. And the only thing I had to break it with was me, so I put me in it."</font> She looked up. <font color=moccasin>"That's not brave. That's just — that's the only tool in the bag."</font>

<font color=royalblue>"Four,"</font> said Iuno.

<font color=moccasin>"Was that four? I thought that was still three."</font>

<font color=royalblue>"That was three and four."</font>

<font color=moccasin>"You can't do *two at once* — "</font>

<font color=royalblue>"I did them at once because you answered them at once, which is a thing you do, and it has cost me a great deal of paper over the years."</font>

## FOUR DOZEN

<font color=royalblue>"Egla."</font>

<font color=moccasin>"Mm."</font>

<font color=royalblue>"The bakery. You went in alone."</font>

<font color=moccasin>"I wanted a cake."</font>

Iuno waited.

<font color=moccasin>"They do a lemon one,"</font> Mei said. <font color=moccasin>"With the sugar on top. The crunchy — the big sugar."</font>

Iuno waited.

<font color=moccasin>"You're doing the wait."</font>

<font color=royalblue>"I am sitting in a chair."</font>

<font color=moccasin>"You're doing the *wait*, you've got the — you've got the *Senate face* on — "</font>

<font color=royalblue>"I have one face."</font>

<font color=moccasin>"That's *Ciaccona's*."</font>

<font color=royalblue>"It is a good line and it is in circulation."</font>

Mei put her hands over her own face and made a noise like a kettle, and then took them away and said it.

<font color=moccasin>"She'd gone small."</font>

<font color=royalblue>"…"</font>

<font color=moccasin>"When she ordered. She stood in the lane and she gave me the order and her voice came out about six years old. I've never heard it before or since. She said *lemon ones, with the sugar,* like she was asking permission off somebody who'd said no before."</font> Mei's jaw moved. <font color=moccasin>"And I thought: right, well, whatever's about to happen in that shop, it's not happening to *that*."</font>

<font color=royalblue>"You went in to take the flinch."</font>

<font color=moccasin>"If there was one going."</font>

<font color=royalblue>"And if there had been."</font>

<font color=moccasin>"Then I'd have bought the cakes and come out and said the queue was long and we'd have gone."</font>

<font color=royalblue>"You would have lied to her."</font>

<font color=moccasin>"Course I'd have lied to her. She'd had twenty years of the truth and it hadn't done her any good."</font> Mei rolled her shoulder, the one with the scar that doesn't show any more. <font color=moccasin>"Anyway, I stood outside for about forty seconds first."</font>

<font color=royalblue>"Doing what?"</font>

<font color=moccasin>"Trying to work out how to say it. Which — there isn't a way. There's no sentence. *Hello, the dead saint's outside and she's forty now and she isn't* — there's no — "</font>

<font color=royalblue>"And then."</font>

<font color=moccasin>"And then Rosa said it first."</font>

Mei went quiet for a second, in the specific way she does over other people's good luck.

<font color=moccasin>"She looked at me for about a second and a half and she got the whole thing off my face, and she went and got the flour off her hands, and she said *Gram kept the recipe.*"</font> Mei shook her head slowly. <font color=moccasin>"Twenty years. Woman's had a *life*, she got *married*, and the first thing out of her is the recipe."</font>

<font color=royalblue>"And you bought forty-eight cakes."</font>

<font color=moccasin>"Forty-eight is a normal amount of cakes."</font>

<font color=royalblue>"For a garrison."</font>

<font color=moccasin>"It's a *bakery*, Iuiu — you can't walk into a woman's shop after twenty years and buy *two* — "</font>

<font color=royalblue>"Two is the number of people who were going to eat them."</font>

<font color=moccasin>"Two is an *insult*."</font>

<font color=royalblue>"Two is a purchase — "</font>

<font color=moccasin>"You have to buy her *out*."</font> Mei was sitting up now, entirely serious about it, gesturing with a flat hand. <font color=moccasin>"That's the sentence. You can't say the sentence, so you buy the shop out, and she stands there and she counts them into the boxes and she knows what you're saying, and then neither of you has to have said it."</font>

Iuno sat in a chair in a rented room and looked at her for a moment.

<font color=royalblue>"…Yes,"</font> she said. <font color=royalblue>"All right."</font>

<font color=moccasin>"Ha."</font>

<font color=royalblue>"Do not."</font>

<font color=moccasin>"You said *all right*, that's — that's a *concession*, that's on the record — "</font>

<font color=royalblue>"Five,"</font> said Iuno.

## FIVE. THE MUSIC.

<font color=royalblue>"The harbour, the night before the square. She walked out to turn herself in and left you her sheet music."</font>

<font color=moccasin>"Yeah."</font>

<font color=royalblue>"You went in after her."</font>

<font color=moccasin>"We went in after her."</font>

<font color=royalblue>"Why."</font>

<font color=moccasin>"Because she left the music."</font>

Iuno's eyebrows went up about a millimetre, which from her is a shout.

<font color=moccasin>"A bard who means it burns the music,"</font> Mei said. <font color=moccasin>"That's — you don't leave your father's *paper* on a table for somebody if you're finished. That's not a goodbye, that's a — "</font> She hunted for it. <font color=moccasin>" — that's a forwarding address."</font>

<font color=royalblue>"That is a clerk's reading."</font>

<font color=moccasin>"That's a *leaver's* reading."</font>

The room did the thing rooms do.

Mei heard it land and let it stand there, which is new, and put her hands behind her on the quilt.

<font color=moccasin>"I know how people go,"</font> she said. <font color=moccasin>"I've gone. You leave the thing you want somebody to come and get. That's the whole trick. You leave it where they'll trip on it and then you tell yourself you didn't ask."</font>

<font color=royalblue>"…"</font>

<font color=moccasin>"Don't file that."</font>

<font color=royalblue>"I am filing that."</font>

<font color=moccasin>"Iuiu — "</font>

<font color=royalblue>"I am filing it in the same place I file the coat and the goose and every other thing you say sideways on your way out of a sentence, and I will bring it up in about a fortnight, and you will pretend not to remember saying it."</font> Iuno's mouth moved, barely. <font color=royalblue>"Six."</font>

<font color=moccasin>"Six is what?"</font>

<font color=royalblue>"Six is the square."</font>

Mei's whole body changed. It was a small change and it went through everything.

<font color=royalblue>"You let her sing."</font>

<font color=moccasin>"…Yeah."</font>

<font color=royalblue>"You knew what was in that crowd. You knew what an acolyte does with a heretic's name and you had a good idea what fifty thousand pairs of eyes were going to do to a woman with a laurel on her head, and you let a bard climb up on a box in the open and sing a banned poem at a theocracy."</font>

<font color=moccasin>"I stood where she could see me."</font>

Iuno stopped.

She stopped completely, in the chair, with her hand halfway to the cup.

<font color=moccasin>"…What?"</font>

<font color=royalblue>"Say that again."</font>

<font color=moccasin>"I — that's it. That's all I did. Front left, by the fish stall, where the — because you can lose a face in a crowd that size and she'd have been up there on her own with the whole city looking at her, and I wanted there to be one place she could look."</font> Mei shrugged, uncomfortable now. <font color=moccasin>"That's not — that's not a *thing*, Iuiu, that's just standing."</font>

<font color=royalblue>"Say it again."</font>

<font color=moccasin>"I stood where she could see me. What?"</font>

Iuno sat back.

## NINE

The curtain came in about two inches and went back.

<font color=royalblue>"Yes,"</font> Iuno said. <font color=royalblue>"Thank you. It was cold on purpose."</font>

The curtain did nothing at all, in a way that had begun to feel deliberate.

*Ten,* said the High Priestess.

*Nine.*

*Ten, darling.*

Iuno lay very still.

*I took one out before I gave you the list,* said the High Priestess, from the velvet lounge, without her feet up, in the voice she manages perhaps four times a year. *You were in the frostlands and you were holding a stump together with your teeth and you were not ready for it, and I have reviewed the matter twice since and I have not changed my assessment.*

*…Which one.*

*Mm,* said the High Priestess.

*Which one.*

*Go to sleep, darling.*

Out past the ward wall the loose shutter kept its four-count, and about an hour later, at the hour Iuno's elbow had been advertising since the second, the weather came down off the plain and went along the terraces and turned the lamp on the sill about a degree, and stood it back up.

In the morning the cup was where she had put it, empty, on the sill.

It had been turned a quarter-turn by the rim.

Neither of them had done it, and neither of them said so.
