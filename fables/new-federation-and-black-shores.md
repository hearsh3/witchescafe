# New Federation and the Black Shores

*Wuthering Waves lore reference for Wuwa The Fables. Includes story spoilers through Version 3.8.*

## New Federation

The New Federation is the successor to the **Old Federation** on Solaris-3. Its present identity grew out of the Resonator War. Scientific talent can change someone's standing rapidly, while aristocrats, corporate interests, and an excluded underclass occupy very different positions in society. Mortefi, himself from the aristocracy, remembers researchers who valued progress so highly that they lost sight of what their inventions were for. [New Federation](https://wutheringwaves.fandom.com/wiki/New_Federation)

### Old Federation and the Resonator War

The Old Federation comprised the **Federated Cities** and provinces such as the **Fifth Sector**. During the **Resonator War**, the Resonator-aligned **Federated Army** fought the country's non-Resonator forces. The Army held the Federated Cities, which sent aid by rail to the Fifth Sector. **Lily**, a young perfumer conscripted on the Resonator side, served with a support team sent there. Her dream-inducing perfumes helped her care for soldiers as a psychotherapist. The story shows bombarded cities, while Lily's character stories follow the soldiers she cared for in a field hospital near the front. [Resonator War](https://wutheringwaves.fandom.com/wiki/Resonator_War) · [New Federation history](https://wutheringwaves.fandom.com/wiki/New_Federation#History) · [Lily's character stories](https://gachaflex.com/wuwa/characters/lily)

In **A Bouquet for Tomorrow** (Chapter IV, Act V), a man from the New Federation comes through a mysterious door into Xuanfang and reports that the old war has resurfaced within the **Somnoire**. Rover boards the **Train of Souls**, a wartime troop train transformed into a dreamlike refuge. Its passengers have lost most of their memories and want to go home. Through Lily and the soldiers' dreams, the quest revisits what the war did to the people who fought it. [Kuro Games’ Version 3.8 notes](https://wutheringwaves.kurogames.com/en/main/news/detail/5783) · [Quest playthrough](https://zolotube.com/watch?v=oTqaLalwGjw)

### The postwar state, cities, and the Lawless Zone

- **Political order.** The Old Federation dissolved after the war, and a new federal government took its place. The wiki describes the resulting state as a capitalist technocracy with strong corporate control. It treats the non-Resonator victory as the likely outcome of the war. A film discussed in Startorch Academy's anecdotes depicts the **capital** under a corrupt corporate conglomerate. The formal relationship between the conglomerate and the federal government remains unclear in these accounts. [New Federation history](https://wutheringwaves.fandom.com/wiki/New_Federation#History) · [Government](https://wutheringwaves.fandom.com/wiki/New_Federation#Government)
- **Cities and environment.** The modern nation has a central capital and an unspecified number of other cities, surrounded by extensive deserts. The **Federated Cities** and **Fifth Sector** are attested in the Old Federation era. The **Lawless Zone** is an impoverished badlands region at the margins of the modern Federation. [Geography](https://wutheringwaves.fandom.com/wiki/New_Federation#Geography) · [Federated Cities](https://wutheringwaves.fandom.com/wiki/Federated_Cities) · [Fifth Sector](https://wutheringwaves.fandom.com/wiki/Fifth_Sector)
- **Exiles and mercenaries.** Barred from legitimate employment, Exiles built camps and formed gangs in the Lawless Zone. In a later conflict, the government waged war against those gangs until **Calcharo's Ghost Hounds** absorbed the last holdout, the **Underdogs**. The state now hires mercenaries from the same region for covert work. **Lynae** worked as a government-contracted assassin before starting over at Startorch Academy. [Lawless Zone](https://wutheringwaves.fandom.com/wiki/Lawless_Zone) · [Calcharo's backstory](https://wutheringwaves.fandom.com/wiki/Calcharo/Backstory) · [What Burns Beneath Frostlands](https://wutheringwaves.fandom.com/wiki/What_Burns_Beneath_Frostlands)
- **Resonator prejudice.** Sue White recounts that her parents told her to stay away after Startorch accepted her. The wiki links such prejudice to the apparent defeat of the Federated Army. Sue's account establishes how it can play out within a family. [New Federation Era](https://wutheringwaves.fandom.com/wiki/New_Federation#New_Federation_Era)

### Culture, science, and the economy

- **Science and industry.** The annual **World Expo** showcases international research. Aircraft, advanced cameras, **Triumph Avionics**, **Panovista Transit**, and **Ashridge Energy** give a sense of the Federation's industrial reach. **Director Chase** worked at Ashridge before heading the Spacetrek Collective. The Montelli patriarch learned Terminal modification in the Federation and built the family's Reserved Terminal business on it. [New Federation](https://wutheringwaves.fandom.com/wiki/New_Federation) · [World Expo](https://wutheringwaves.fandom.com/wiki/World_Expo)
- **Academia and medicine.** **Lucilla** edited the country's leading academic journal before becoming Startorch Academy's president. Federation universities use thesis defenses, and Anyad encountered a mismatch with Startorch's Synchronist program. **Luuk Herssen** formerly led the **Novialle Regenerative Group**, whose pharmaceutical innovation **Ichor** is controversial. The updated nation entry names **Promeus** as its Sentinel. Galilei describes the capital's use of Sentinel research as exploitation. [New Federation Era](https://wutheringwaves.fandom.com/wiki/New_Federation#New_Federation_Era) · [All That Sunlight Touches](https://wutheringwaves.fandom.com/wiki/All_That_Sunlight_Touches)
- **Art and everyday life.** Federation scholars study the origins of Rinascitan drama, and experimental works such as *The Last Jar of Air Before the Lament* attract Rinascitan interest. **Karl** wrote the popular *Second Coming of Solaris*. Federation television has an audience at Startorch, where Lucilla keeps copies of the old films she brought from home, which also received a sample of pre-Lament lunar soil. Verina recalls expensive flowers and elaborate competitions. [Culture and Economy](https://wutheringwaves.fandom.com/wiki/New_Federation#Culture_and_Economy) · [Lucilla](https://wutheringwaves.fandom.com/wiki/Lucilla)
- **Finance and the Fractsidus.** Federation firms acquired several Rinascitan companies through after-hours trading, and those companies approached Black Shores' **KU-Money** for help. The Fractsidus Vice Architect **Eris** directs important financial operations from the Federation through agents in business, government, and elite society. Eris's identity remains unknown. [KU-Money](https://wutheringwaves.fandom.com/wiki/KU-Money) · [Eris](https://wutheringwaves.fandom.com/wiki/%22Eris%22) · [All That Sunlight Touches](https://wutheringwaves.fandom.com/wiki/All_That_Sunlight_Touches)

Other Federation figures include **Mornye** and **Verina**, who appear in the stories of Startorch Academy and Jinzhou respectively. **Dr. Scott Rabelle** was born in the Federation and later settled in Lilyland. The country is also home to a Tacetite-eating Wyrm. [New Federation](https://wutheringwaves.fandom.com/wiki/New_Federation)

## The Black Shores

The **Black Shores** are a hidden archipelago in the **Stormy Sea** and the headquarters of an international organization founded by **Rover** to combat the Lament. The **Shorekeeper** guards the islands and oversees operations in Rover's absence. The organization collects data, forecasts disasters, and helps partners with evacuation and reconstruction. Those partners include Jinzhou, the Montelli family, the Ephor's Palace, and Startorch Academy. The Fractsidus are its adversaries. [Black Shores faction](https://wutheringwaves.fandom.com/wiki/Black_Shores) · [Black Shores location](https://wutheringwaves.fandom.com/wiki/The_Black_Shores) · [Staff handbook](https://wutheringwaves.fandom.com/wiki/Basic_Introduction_to_the_Black_Shores_II)

### Founding and story

- **Before the main story.** The **Tethys System** predates the Lament. Its origin is uncertain. Rover founded the Black Shores around the work of understanding and forecasting the catastrophe. The Shorekeeper took human form after Rover's frequency resonated with the Remnant Energy crystal in which she originated. [Tethys System](https://wutheringwaves.fandom.com/wiki/Tethys_System) · [Shorekeeper's backstory](https://wutheringwaves.fandom.com/wiki/Shorekeeper/Backstory) · [Timeline](https://wutheringwaves.fandom.com/wiki/Timeline)
- **Rewinding Raindrops.** In Jinzhou, **Aalto** and **Encore** help the amnesiac Rover follow the trail of the Blake Bloom. They make a deal involving a Resonance Beacon in the Court of Savantae ruins. Aalto then reveals that Rover previously awoke at the Black Shores. **Camellya**, who has been watching Rover, is also a member. [Rewinding Raindrops](https://wuwastory.com/story/rewinding-raindrops/)
- **To the Shore's End.** Rover investigates an abnormal frequency tied to the destruction of the **Port City of Guixu**. **Hoda**, a Bloom Bearer and field medic who died there, is among the people whose frequencies remain trapped in the **Necrostar**. The Shorekeeper becomes Tethys's computational core during the crisis. Rover intervenes to free her and release the trapped souls. Afterward, Tethys operates on a replacement frequency with properties similar to the Lament. [To the Shore's End](https://wuthering.th.gl/db/stories/story_100012) · [Beyond the Shore's End](https://wutheringwaves.fandom.com/wiki/Beyond_the_Shore%27s_End)
- **Honami and the Chronorift.** A later Black Shores investigation discovers a rift into a **Sonoro Sphere** containing **Honami**, a ruined city caught in a repeating Lament. Rover and **Chisa** investigate the loop in Version 2.8. Honami retains traces of its former civic life in its cafés, cherry-blossom residential quarter, football stadium, and exhibition spaces. [Kuro Games’ Version 2.8 preview](https://blog.playstation.com/?p=412034) · [Chronorift Metropolis](https://wutheringwaves.fandom.com/wiki/Chronorift_Metropolis)

### Culture and life at headquarters

- **Joining and belonging.** The organization recruits internationally. **Consultants** such as Aalto, Encore, Galbrena, and Buling work abroad. **Bloom Bearers** usually work at headquarters. **Gardeners** screen recruits for tailored **germination tests**, and Camellya handles combat tests. Members wear a **Blake Bloom**, a Remnant flower native to the islands, as a sign of membership. The archipelago's Tacetite fog distorts perception and disrupts communications. Recognized or invited visitors need guidance, and the Shores use a Beacon relay to reach the outside world. [Black Shores faction](https://wutheringwaves.fandom.com/wiki/Black_Shores) · [Paranora](https://wutheringwaves.fandom.com/wiki/Paranora) · [Camellya's profile](https://wutheringwaves.notion.site/Introduction-14bef7f91e4b8179abd9f6e8ab55a88f) · [Wutherium Geographic](https://wutheringwaves.notion.site/Wutherium-Geographic-Lands-and-Waters-The-Black-Shores-Vol-1-c2f73ba68c994c088cef21ed4e0180bc)
- **The Greenhouse.** Newcomers are invited to plant something, often from home. Aalto chose a money tree, Camellya planted a whole garden, and the robot Gardeners suggest a Blake Bloom to Rover, who cannot remember a hometown. **KU-Plant** and **KU-Water** tend the greenhouse, which also houses a piano used as a modulation device. KU robots work throughout the Shores as Tethys terminals and have distinct personalities. [Greenhouse](https://wutheringwaves.fandom.com/wiki/Greenhouse) · [The Greenhouse and the Modulation Piano](https://wutheringwaves.notion.site/To-Shore-s-End-The-Greenhouse-and-the-Modulation-Piano-1e6ef7f91e4b808cafa8fbad883c5356) · [Black Shores faction](https://wutheringwaves.fandom.com/wiki/Black_Shores)
- **Off duty.** The **Haven of Sprouts** is the staff's living quarters. It has arcade machines and collections brought from around the world. Staff use doors for quick travel between facilities, and tell a story about a mysterious door of their own. [Haven of Sprouts](https://wutheringwaves.fandom.com/wiki/Haven_of_Sprouts)
- **The work.** Field teams and **Resonance Beacons** supply data for Tethys. The system models Lament events in **Stellar Matrices** and can issue warnings roughly **24–48 hours ahead**. The archipelago's Tacetite supplies energy. The headquarters has space for research, housing, preparation, and receiving foreign envoys. [Tethys System](https://wutheringwaves.fandom.com/wiki/Tethys_System) · [Black Shores Archipelago](https://wutheringwaves.fandom.com/wiki/Black_Shores_Archipelago) · [Staff handbook](https://wutheringwaves.fandom.com/wiki/Basic_Introduction_to_the_Black_Shores_II)
